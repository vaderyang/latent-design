Attention follows understanding, not activity.

Today's agentic interfaces spend attention rendering tool calls — searching, reading, executing. But the tool call is the theater of cognition, not cognition itself. This design language reallocates the user's finite attention to the latent structure of the problem, and to the agent's evolving, evidence-grounded understanding of it.

现有的 agentic 界面把注意力花在渲染工具调用上——搜索中、读取中、执行中。但工具调用是认知的剧场，不是认知本身。这套设计语言把用户有限的注意力，重新分配给问题的潜结构、以及 agent 对它不断演化的、被证据锚定的理解。

The Inversion
立论 / The Inversion

Invert the visual hierarchy

把视觉层级倒过来

Almost every product in the field — including the so-called frontier of Generative UI — is fundamentally tool-call-rooted: the UI is generated from tool output, and reasoning is squeezed into a caption beside the action, an after-the-fact justification. 潛 inverts this hierarchy. Actions recede into a peripheral, retrievable trace; the agent's evolving understanding takes the main stage.

领域里几乎所有产品——包括被视为最前沿的 Generative UI——本质上都是 tool-call-rooted：UI 的生成源头是工具的输出，推理被压成动作旁边的一行小字、一个事后的辩护。潛 把这个层级倒过来。动作退为外围、可调取的痕迹；agent 不断演化的理解占据主舞台。

The Grounding Contract
认识论纪律 / The Grounding Contract

The backbone against reasoning theater

对抗 reasoning theater 的脊梁

This is what sets 潛 apart from every existing approach, and it is its safety foundation. The rendered latent is not the real latent — chain-of-thought is often post-hoc rationalization, and the prettier it looks, the more easily it gilds unfaithful cognition with the gold leaf of "credible." Therefore: only falsifiable, evidence-grounded cognition earns the main attention. Any element that wants the main stage must be one of three legitimate forms.

这是潛 区别于一切现有方案的核心，也是它的安全底座。渲染出来的 latent 不等于真实的 latent——链式推理常是事后合理化，做得越漂亮越容易给不忠实的认知贴上“可信”的金箔。因此：只有可证伪、被证据锚定的认知，才配占据主注意力。任何想进入主舞台的元素，必须是以下三种合法形态之一。

◆ Grounded Claim

Grounded conclusion

已锚定结论

An assertion backed by evidence. It carries the highest visual weight — gold amber, meaning "landed, verifiable."

一个被证据支撑的论断。占据最高视觉权重——金 amber，意为“已落地、可验证”。

Must carry: click-through provenance pointing to evidence / observable primitives
必须携带：指向 evidence / observable primitives 的可点开溯源
◇ Hypothesis

Hypothesis in play

在押假设

A belief held with uncertainty. Aqua, meaning "in the water, under investigation." Always shows confidence.

一个被以不确定性持有的信念。青 aqua，意为“在水中、探查中”。永远显示置信度。

Must carry: confidence + "what evidence would change it"
必须携带：置信度 + “什么证据会改变它”
○ Open Question

An honest unknown

诚实的未知

Something the agent explicitly does not know. Indigo, low weight but visible — hiding the unknown is itself a form of theater.

一个 agent 明确不知道的事。靛 indigo，低权重但可见——隐藏未知本身就是一种 theater。

Must carry: the open item / what it would take to resolve it
必须携带：待查项 / 解决它需要什么

This contract is precisely your five-layer diagnostic ontology: the upper ontological layers (hypotheses, conclusions) are the on-stage subject, but each must have a thread tying it back to the lower-layer Observable Primitives. Reasoning theater is dangerous precisely because its narrative floats free; the Grounding Contract keeps free-floating assertions off the main stage by demanding "must be falsifiable, must land on evidence."

这条契约正是你的五层诊断本体论：本体论上层（假设、结论）是台前主体，但每一个都必须有一根线栓回下层的 Observable Primitives。Reasoning theater 之所以危险，正因为它的叙述悬空；Grounding Contract 用“必须可证伪、必须落到证据”把悬空的断言挡在主舞台之外。

Three voices

三种声音

Typography divides labor by role: the agent's cognitive "voice," the interface's functional text, and evidence / provenance read as instrument output. The voice is serif, because thinking should look deliberated; evidence is monospace, because it should look like a verifiable instrument reading.

Principles
原则 / Principles

Seven principles

七条原则

01

Attention follows understanding, not activity

注意力跟随理解，而非活动

The single root law. Visual weight goes to "what was understood / what changed in the understanding," not "which step executed." Everything else follows from this.

唯一的根定律。视觉权重分配给“理解了什么 / 理解发生了什么变化”，而非“执行了哪一步”。一切其余皆由此推出。

02

The main stage admits only falsifiable cognition

主舞台只接纳可证伪的认知

The Grounding Contract. An element entering the main attention must be one of: a grounded claim, a hypothesis (with confidence and a falsification condition), or an open question. Free-floating assertions are kept out — this is the backbone against reasoning theater.

Grounding Contract。进入主注意力的元素必须是 grounded claim、hypothesis（带置信度与可证伪条件）、或 open question 三者之一。悬空断言被挡在门外——这是对抗 reasoning theater 的脊梁。

03

Surface inflections, sink narration

浮现拐点，沉降叙述

Prioritize highlighting backtracks / ahas / refutations — research confirms these are the most faithful, highest-information parts of the latent. Sink procedural narration like "I'm reading a file" (the breeding ground of unfaithfulness) to the periphery.

优先高亮 backtrack / 啊哈 / 推翻——研究证实这些是 latent 中最忠实、信息量最高的部分。把“我在读文件”这类程序性叙述（不忠实的温床）沉到外围。

04

Recede ≠ hide

弱化 ≠ 隐藏

Tool calls step back visually, but provenance is always one click away and reconstructible. At a financial customer, "which data did the agent touch" is a compliance question; auditability is non-negotiable.

工具调用视觉退后，但 provenance 永远一键可调、可重建。在金融客户处，“agent 碰了哪些数据”是合规问题，审计能力不可让渡。

05

Uncertainty is first-class content

不确定性是一等内容

Confidence, open questions, and downgrade rationales are rendered explicitly, not smoothed away. An honest "I'm not sure" is more credible — and safer — than a confident error.

置信度、open question、降级理由都被显式渲染，不被抹平。诚实的“我不确定”比自信的错误更可信，也更安全。

06

Understanding persists, activity is transient

理解态持久，活动短暂

Actions flow past and vanish; the understanding state remains, accumulates, and can be revisited. This is exactly the object users "come back to check" in the era of ambient / background agents.

动作流过即逝；理解态留存、累积、可回访。这正是 ambient / 后台 agent 时代用户“回来查看”的那个对象。

07

Two personas, two views

两种人格，两种视图

The Operator view (latent-dominant, default) serves the end user; the Trace view (tool-call-dominant) serves debugging and audit. One toggle dissolves the "transparency vs. cognition-first" tension, rather than forcing an either-or.

Operator 视图（latent 主导，默认）服务终端用户；Trace 视图（tool-call 主导）服务调试与审计。一个 toggle 化解“透明 vs 认知优先”的张力，而非二选一。

Voice
语态 / Voice

Epistemic honesty, not theater

认识论诚实，而非剧场

The design language also governs how the agent speaks. The 潛 voice refuses the "working hard" performance, and refuses to dress post-hoc narration up as insight. It states confidence, flags the unknown, and stays honest about "the gap between narration and computation."

设计语言也约束 agent 怎么说话。潛 的语态拒绝“正在努力工作”的表演，也拒绝把事后叙述伪装成洞见。它陈述置信、标注未知、对“叙述与计算之间的落差”保持诚实。

Anti-patterns
治理 / Anti-patterns

Adoption red lines

采纳红线

When any team adopts 潛, the following are anti-patterns that quietly betray the core of the language. They look like "showing the reasoning," but in fact they rebuild the theater.

任何团队接入潛 时，以下是会悄悄背叛该语言核心的反模式。它们看起来像“在展示推理”，实则在重建剧场。

Pour raw CoT straight onto the main stage
把 CoT 原文直接灌进主舞台
Raw reasoning unfiltered by the Grounding Contract is a breeding ground for reasoning theater. It must be parsed into the grounded / hypothesis / open three states.
未经 Grounding Contract 过滤的 raw 推理是 reasoning theater 的温床。必须解析为 grounded/hypothesis/open 三态。
Use spinners and progress bars to fake "being busy"
用 spinner 和进度条制造“在忙”的假象
Activity volume ≠ understanding. Upgrading activity theater to cognition theater is no improvement — it just swaps the backdrop.
活动量 ≠ 理解。把活动剧场升级为认知剧场没有改善，只是换了块幕布。
Turn confidence into ungrounded "vibe numbers"
把置信度做成无依据的“氛围数字”
Confidence must hang on "what evidence supports / refutes it," otherwise it's just gilding random numbers.
置信必须挂在“什么证据支撑/反驳它”上，否则就是给随机数贴金箔。
Delete refuted hypotheses for the sake of "cleanliness"
为了“干净”而删除被推翻的假设
Sink, don't delete. Both audit and trust depend on the traceability of "what the model considered, and then ruled out."
沉降而非删除。审计与信任都依赖“模型考虑过、又排除了什么”的可追溯。
Force a hypothesis board onto low-latent tasks
给低 latent 任务硬套假设板
Violates proportionality. Bolting a diagnostic interface onto "rewrite this sentence" only creates noise and a sense of performance.
违反比例原则。诊断界面套在“改写一句话”上只会制造噪声与表演感。
Fold provenance into a dead link that can't be retrieved
把 provenance 折成无法调取的死链
Recede ≠ hide. The moment provenance can't be reconstructed, the whole language is bankrupt on compliance.
弱化 ≠ 隐藏。一旦 provenance 不可重建，整套语言在合规上就破产了。
